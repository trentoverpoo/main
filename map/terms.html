Accuracy, and the limits of it

Every entity and connection cites a document, and the build fails rather than warns if a citation points at a file that is not in this repository. That check guarantees the citation, not the underlying record: public filings contain errors, records go stale, and a reading of a document can be wrong. The site is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, and nothing here is legal, financial, investment or professional advice. Verify anything you intend to rely on against the primary record.
