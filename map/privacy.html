Privacy

The short version: this site collects nothing about you. It is a static page — HTML, CSS and JavaScript files and a data file, served as they are. There is no account, no login, no form, no server of ours to send anything to.

Last updated 18 September 2026

What is not collected

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No cookies.
  • No advertising, and no third-party advertising or data-broker services.
  • No account, sign-up, contact form or newsletter.
  • Nothing you type into the search or filter boxes leaves your browser. The map data is loaded with the page and searched locally on your own device.

What stays on your device

The site remembers one thing, and it stays in your own browser's local storage: whether you chose the light or dark theme. It is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Hosting

The site is published as static files through GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers process the ordinary request information needed to deliver a page — for example your IP address and browser user agent — under its own policies, which are outside this project's control. This project does not receive, request or keep those logs.

Links off this site

Source citations may link to outside pages — a filing index, an agency record, a published article. Once you follow such a link you are on someone else's site, under their privacy practices, not this one's.

If you send a tip

Nothing on this site sends a tip for you. The tipline is an ordinary email address. If you choose to write, you are sending an email, and it carries whatever your own mail provider attaches to it — your address included. Tips are handled in confidence, and nothing you send is published or attributed to you without your explicit permission. Use an anonymous mailbox if that matters to you.

People named on the map

Entities and people appear on this map on the strength of public records — filings, deeds, permits, court documents and published pages. If a record about you here is wrong, out of date or missing context, write to trentoverpoo@proton.me and it will be checked against the document and corrected if it is wrong.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version replaces this page and the date above changes with it. The page history is public in the project's repository.
